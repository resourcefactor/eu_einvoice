The **E-Invoicing** workspace shows number cards and charts for e-invoiced Sales Invoices and E Invoice Imports. All figures respect the user's permissions, e.g. a user restricted to one company only sees that company's numbers.

- **E Invoice Status**: Sales Invoices of the companies enabled in **EU Einvoice Settings** with their e-invoice status (Valid, Invalid, Not Validated, Not E-Invoiced), validation errors and whether the XML is attached.
- **E Invoice Import Register**: Imported supplier e-invoices with their validation result and the linked **Purchase Invoice**.
